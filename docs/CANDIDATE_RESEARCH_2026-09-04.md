## DELL — the desk view is stale, not wrong

Desk: sleeper, grade D, "near resistance, far from support," 85 days to
earnings. What changed: Dell reported FQ2'27 on Sep 1 — revenue $46.97B vs
$44.9B consensus, adj. EPS $7.04 vs $4.91, AI-server revenue ~$16.4B, record
$60.9B AI orders, backlog ~$95B, FY27 guide raised to ~$192B / $25.50 EPS from
$167B / $17.90. Stock +15.8% on Sep 2 on ~4.5x volume; wave of target raises
(JPM $635, Melius $735). Forward P/E ~18x, well above Dell's own 10–15x history.

Bear case being argued: FCF fell 47% YoY to ~$1.0B on working-capital build,
negative shareholders' equity, low-margin AI boxes priced like a platform,
concentration in a few neocloud/sovereign deals.

Read-through for the desk: the "sleeper" label and the straddle default no
longer fit — the event just happened and implied vol has been crushed. The
desk's own citation [BT89] (post-earnings-announcement drift) is the relevant
frame now: this is a **drift / follow-through** candidate, not an event-vol
candidate. Resistance at $530.78 is the old 52-week high; a clean break and
hold above it is the confirmation the watchlist brief asks for.

What would upgrade it: (1) close above $531 with two weeks of holding above the
Sep 2 close (~$492); (2) ISG margin guide (15%) surviving the next data points;
(3) any FCF recovery commentary. What would kill it: gap fill below $492 on
rising volume, or memory-cost inflation hitting AI-server margins.

## DDOG — the thesis is being tested right now

Desk: bearish trend, conviction 30 (lowest of the four), "high PE, options
setup thin." Outside evidence: Q2 (Aug 6) beat — revenue $1.12B (+36%),
EPS $0.65 vs $0.58, RPO +43% — but management disclosed its largest AI-native
customer (widely reported as OpenAI) renewed a nine-figure deal **and will cut
usage from Q3**. Q3 guide steps growth down to 28–29%. Stock fell ~17% that
day and is −22% over the month; it now sits at the desk's $208 support.
Consensus is still Strong Buy (avg PT ~$285), Guggenheim Sell, Bernstein
Market Perform. Forward P/S ~18x, forward P/E ~80x.

Near-term dated catalysts: Citi Global TMT conference **Sep 8**, Goldman
Communacopia **Sep 10**, Q3 print ~Nov 5.

Read-through: this is the one name where the desk's low conviction is
*corroborated* by fresh evidence (customer concentration, decel, premium
multiple). Being at support is not a thesis. Straddle is again the wrong
default — the vol event is two months away.

What would upgrade it: hold $208 through the two conferences with commentary
that non-AI-native growth (~85% of revenue) is intact; or a Q3 print that
beats the derisked 28–29% guide. What would kill it: a daily close below
$208 → next reference is the 52-week low region.

## CRWV — "accumulate only on weakness" is now live, so the risk question matters

Desk: inventory candidate, grade C, S $79 / R $117, "accumulate only on
weakness." Outside evidence: Q2 (Aug 11) revenue $2.6B (+112%), backlog
$104B (+246%) plus ~$25B new commitments, FY26 guide raised to $12.4–13.2B.
But: interest expense $640M (2.4x YoY), GAAP loss $626M, $2.6B new
delayed-draw facility at SOFR+550 with a tenor longer than typical customer
contracts, >$30B raised YTD, −12% on Aug 18 as yields spiked, an executive
10b5-1 sale, stock −43% from its high. Avg PT ~$144 (38 analysts). EV/Sales
~12x trailing. Total-debt figures conflict across sources ($14B–$52B incl.
leases) — unresolved.

Read-through: price ($84.6) is inside the "weakness" zone the desk said it
wanted. The thing to be honest about is **beta 7.4** and a leverage story tied
to the rate path: on a ~$1k NLV account, share accumulation of an $85 stock
with that beta is not a defined-risk exercise. If this becomes a paper
candidate it belongs in a defined-risk structure sized off max loss, and the
desk's construction cap ($250–500) already says so.

What would upgrade it: holds $79 support; evidence of non-Microsoft customer
mix; borrowing costs stabilising; backlog conversion running ahead of ~12%/yr.
What would kill it: loss of $79 on volume, or any financing headline that
widens spreads on its debt.

## AEHR — nearest catalyst, thinnest margin for error

Desk: sleeper, ready 93 (highest of the four), RVOL 1.88x, S $74 / R $147, ~35
days to earnings. Outside evidence: FQ4'26 (Jul 14) revenue $18.8M, record
bookings $60.7M, effective backlog ~$100M, FY27 revenue guide $130–150M
(+160–200%), ~70% of that from AI-processor wafer-level burn-in at one lead
customer; $22M follow-on order (Aug 12) and a silicon-photonics production
order (Aug 4). Stock ran to ~$93 post-print and has faded to $76.3, −5% today,
**sitting on the desk's $74.23 support.** 5 analysts, avg PT ~$130; ~14% of
float short. Forward P/E ~100x; ~18x EV/Sales on FY27 guidance.

Read-through: this is the only one of the four where the straddle default and
the calendar line up — an event inside ~31 days, high RVOL, huge guide
dependency on one customer, and a crowded short. It is also the smallest and
most concentrated: one customer decides whether the guide is real.

What would upgrade it: holds $74 into the print; any *third* named customer or
HBM order (not in guide); the Jefferies / Needham semicap conference dates
firming up as pre-print read-throughs. What would kill it: close below $74
ahead of earnings (the setup then becomes a breakdown, not a sleeper).

## Ranking, in the desk's own terms

1. **AEHR** — highest readiness, nearest dated catalyst, structure matches the
   desk default. Best fit for the paper lane *if* $74 holds. Highest
   single-name risk.
2. **DELL** — strongest fundamentals of the four, but the event is behind it;
   the drift frame beats the straddle frame. Re-score with technicals once the
   post-gap range settles.
3. **CRWV** — thesis is intact and price is where the desk wanted it, but
   leverage + beta 7 make it a defined-risk-only conversation.
4. **DDOG** — fresh evidence confirms the low conviction; wait for the
   Sep 8/10 conference read-through before spending research time.
