# The Growth Roadmap — how the base actually gets bigger

- **Date:** 2026-07-09
- **Author:** Claude (research lane). Educational/decision-support, not personalized
  financial advice; ~8%/yr is an illustrative long-run index assumption, not a
  promise. This turns the research's recurring conclusion — *grow the base* — into
  concrete numbers and milestones.

## The number that reframes everything

At a $1,100 starting balance, a good market year (~8%) adds about **$88**. Here's
what monthly contributions add in that same first year, and how much more they do:

| Monthly added | Year-1 contributions | vs the ~$88 market return |
|---|---|---|
| $50/mo | $600 | **6.8× the market's help** |
| $100/mo | $1,200 | **13.6×** |
| $200/mo | $2,400 | **27.3×** |

This is the whole reason the strategy hunt was optimizing the wrong variable. Every
clever edge was fighting over the ~8% slice. The contribution *is* the growth, by
an order of magnitude, until the account is much larger.

## Time to reach the milestones that matter (start $1,100, ~8%/yr)

| Monthly added | → $5,000 | → $10,000 | → $25,000 | → $50,000 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $0 (just hold) | 19 yrs | 28 yrs | 39 yrs | 48 yrs |
| $50/mo | 4.8 yrs | 8.9 yrs | 16.8 yrs | 23.8 yrs |
| $100/mo | 2.8 yrs | 5.6 yrs | 11.4 yrs | 17.6 yrs |
| $200/mo | 1.5 yrs | 3.2 yrs | 7.2 yrs | 11.9 yrs |
| $400/mo | 0.8 yrs | 1.8 yrs | 4.2 yrs | 7.4 yrs |

**Read the first row.** With nothing added, $1,100 needs ~39 years to reach the
$25k where any of the options-strategy work even begins to matter. With $200/mo it's
~7 years. The contribution rate, not the return, sets the timeline.

## The plan (concrete, in order)

1. **Automate a contribution you can actually sustain.** Even $50–100/mo changes the
   timeline from "decades" to "years." Automatic transfer on payday so it happens
   before it can be spent.
2. **Put it in one low-cost broad index fund**, zero-commission broker, tiny expense
   ratio (see `ACTION_PLAN_SMALL_ACCOUNT_2026-07-09.md`).
3. **Add the monthly trend overlay only once the balance is meaningful** (say
   >$5–10k), where protecting against a −50% crash actually protects real money. At
   $1,100 the protection is real but the dollars are small; the priority now is
   *contributions*, not risk management.
4. **Revisit the strategy question at ~$25k.** That's where the options/short-premium
   work (already built and waiting) could earn more than its costs. Until then it
   stays a $0 research pursuit, not a spend.

## The honest reframe

You started this looking for an edge to make money fast on a tiny account. The
research's real gift isn't a strategy — it's the proof that you don't need one, and
that chasing one was pulling energy from the thing that actually works: **feeding
the base and letting it compound, protected from ruin.** That's slower and less
exciting than a winning trade, but unlike the trade, it's real, and the math above
is the receipt.
