# Calibration Evidence Contract

## Belief

The existing calibration archive cannot establish predictive edge. Raw row
volume, mutable ranks, and early intrinsic-value labels are not independent
out-of-sample evidence.

## Evidence

On 2026-09-06, 907 finite-R option rows comprised 3 paper and 904 shadow rows.
713 shadow reviews preceded expiration-session open. The archive had 136
source/ticker/expiration exposure groups and 771 repeated exposure rows; groups
are proxies rather than independent events. All historical option rows and
1,958 scenario rows lacked an immutable entry-score snapshot. The old priority
diagnostic put 725 scored option rows into one 0–49 bucket.

## Durable rule

Freeze prediction features when a record is created. Never populate missing
legacy entry features from a later refresh. Keep source-specific denominators,
timing defects, and missingness visible. A descriptive rank is not a probability.
Future shadow intrinsic reviews require an exact-date close from a later history
snapshot. The evaluator and authority code remain outside unattended modification.

## Falsifier

A versioned dataset has immutable entry economics/features, timestamped outcome
prices, event groups and exclusion reasons; a predeclared challenger improves
net-R performance across untouched later-event folds against a simple baseline.
Even then, historical shadow observations do not become paper promotion credit.
