Recorded 2026-09-30. Related: [[Due Work Requires Changed Usable Evidence]].

Mikka authorized operational priorities, assignments, retries and acceptance of tested work. Paper and capital authority remains with existing actors. Margin means usage, cloud cost, duplicated work and progress per run.

Observed baseline: evidence loop reported 2 productive runs among 10 full runs, 294 elapsed seconds and 2 accepted progress points. This identifies a diagnosis task, not a claim of measured savings. Cloud billed dollars are unknown. Account-wide quota is not attributable run cost.

Belief: stable assignments, owner leases, fixed acceptance and bounded unchanged-state retry suppression should reduce duplicate effort without suppressing due work. Falsifier: missed due delivery or unchanged duplicate dispatch while a lease/backoff is active. Measure accepted progress and missed due work over later runs; launch test success alone does not establish improvement.

Durable rule: a new timestamp or exit zero is not accepted progress. Require successful role evidence; code acceptance binds to the current source in isolation. Report recovery earns zero promotion credit. Keep evaluator/authority sources outside unattended modification. External jobs lacking receipts are unverified, never automatically credited. Never extend a retry deadline because a check was skipped.
