# Diagnose the Forward Collection Funnel

Before launching another study, locate the registered protocol and count where
its current candidates stop. A quote fetch, construction, risk gate, approval,
fill and fixed-exit outcome are different stages. Missing observations are not
losses; an empty approved queue is not evidence of no opportunity.

On 2026-09-28 the existing short-premium batch had 40 candidates: 31 pricing
failures and nine construction/economics failures, with no campaign paper rows.
Keep blocked proposals and recorded reasons in the archive. Preserve the
2026-10-05 deadline; do not silently reset an underfilled experiment.

Falsifier: later source hashes and the funnel must show new usable inputs and
source-reconciled cost evidence. A timestamp-only refresh must leave meaningful
state unchanged. Collection diagnostics neither authorize tickets nor redefine
an evaluator's confirmation criteria.
