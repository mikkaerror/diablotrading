# Full industry coverage without eligibility changes

Implemented priority two after metric repairs. `inferno_industry_coverage.py`
joins every current tracker symbol to reference taxonomy and optional dated,
source-linked economic-role interpretations. It performs no top-N selection
and cannot add configured names absent from the tracker.

Initial measurable coverage: 183/183 visible; 24 issuer-linked roles and 159
provider-reference entries. The latter are explicitly due for issuer review.
Never describe all 183 as issuer verified. Missing references remain unresolved
rows, not exclusions. Old edge theme bonuses and classifier are not rewritten
by this research artifact.

Integrated the report in doctor, command-center reporting, and the existing
morning scheduled write-up. The morning schedule, current model and delivery
remain unchanged. Recurring runs read role data but do not change it unattended.

Falsifier: any tracker symbol disappearing due to score/theme, any outside
symbol being added by role configuration, stale sources labeled current, or a
provider-only entry described as issuer verified violates this contract.
Dedicated tests cover these distinctions where applicable. No risk constants,
operator roles, ticket decisions or broker authority changed.
