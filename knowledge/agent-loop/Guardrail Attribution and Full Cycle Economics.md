# Guardrail attribution and full-cycle economics

Source report: September 10 16:29:04 Mountain. The isolated audit preserves
40/183 selected rows and frozen scores, reproduces every saved lane, and removes
one gate at a time. Artifacts: `outputs/guardrail-audit-2026-09-10/`.

## Accepted findings

- Removing only the earnings-window predicate yields four catalyst research
  names; removing each score/confirmation/readiness/trigger/theme predicate
  individually yields none. This supports investigating a separate horizon;
  it is not return evidence or permission to disregard earnings dates.
- Removing only the long-term composite threshold adds four ownership research
  names. Effects overlap and are conditional on prior selection and scores.
- The actual valuation function treats negative P/E like low positive P/E.
  Eight cached names have negative effective P/E; three are in the saved subset.
  Cache at audit time is not proven identical to metadata at scoring time.
- A synthetic past-event row passes the classifier; none of the 40 actual rows
  has negative earnings days. Do not report this as an observed bad ticket.
- Revenue-growth clipping at 50% discards larger growth differences; it remains
  an unvalidated transformation, not proof that removing clipping is better.

## Durable rules and falsifiers

Distinguish protection of capital, quality of execution, and scope of discovery.
Correct metric units/validity before tuning cutoffs. Preserve negative and
missing earnings semantics instead of interpreting them as cheap valuation or
a future catalyst. Require full-universe impact review before production repair.

Count gate admissions as admissions, never winners. Falsify the hypothesis that
a gate change helps by evaluating later after-cost outcomes with all candidate
and rejected names, matched horizons and capital assumptions. Do not rerun the
unchanged frozen audit as new progress.

Track the full cycle from customer economics to value per diluted share. A
supplier's backlog and a customer's capex may describe the same commitment;
they are not independent demand. Funding/delivery delays, weaker utilization,
price compression or dilution can falsify an equity thesis even during revenue
growth. Raw NLV drawdown cannot establish investment return without cash flows.

No runtime functions, risk constants, eligibility, ticket decisions or authority
were changed. Focused and broad tests validate repository integrity, not trading
edge. Next review: nonpositive P/E and forward-event semantics, complete economic
classification, then supplier-versus-developer per-share valuation scenarios.
