# Industry Horizons and Discovery Coverage

## Accepted evidence

The September 10 frozen tracker has 183 unique names. Edge research scores 40
after sorting by readiness, longTermScore and earnings proximity. The saved
40-name set was reproduced exactly without importing production modules.
Conviction retains all 183 names; this is not a full-system row-loss claim.

Only 8/40 edge rows have a classified theme. The 16:30 registry maps 30/183;
the later 17:11 conviction maps 33/183. Preserve their different generation
times. Applying existing category rules to all reference metadata maps 47/183,
leaving 136 unclassified. Removing the row cap alone cannot repair the missing
industry vocabulary.

Among tracker names, 55 lack edge metadata and 48 cached records exceed 14 days.
FIX's September 2 cache has revenueGrowth=0.01, while its July 23 issuer release
reports roughly 50% quarterly revenue growth. Period and definition need
reconciliation. A fetch timestamp does not certify observation freshness.
NVDA's cache is about 128 days old, but it was outside the edge selection; do
not imply that every downstream consumer used that stale value.

## Beliefs and falsifiers

- Connectivity needs qualification, production, customer mix and gross-profit
  tracking. Deferred adoption, socket losses and weak conversion falsify it.
- Electrical equipment and contractors need organic orders, funded backlog,
  commissioning and cash conversion. Persistent delays with deteriorating
  margin and cash weaken the thesis even when backlog increases.
- Cloud capacity needs separate planned, contracted, financed, connected,
  accepted and billable stages. Separate gross MW from IT MW, contracted ARR
  from operating ARR, and prepayments from earnings. Funding cost, dilution and
  missed acceptance dates can overwhelm growth.
- Use event, two-to-eight-quarter operating, and multi-year structural research
  horizons. These are analytical proposals, not runtime or trade instructions.

## Durable rule

Before attributing missing candidates to tight thresholds, measure upstream
coverage and issuer/period consistency. Unclassified is unresolved research,
not proof of no economic exposure. A coverage repair is not proof of predictive
improvement. Keep business growth, expectations, payoff and authority separate.
