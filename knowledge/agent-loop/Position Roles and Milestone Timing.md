# Position roles and milestone timing

The operator requested timing, sizing and long-term strategy after the industry
review. Completed an isolated 17-symbol daily-history comparison, broker-account
reconciliation, standard paired-call-spread analysis and simultaneous-loss
scenarios. Private account inputs and outputs stay under ignored `data/`.

## Beliefs and falsifiers

- Business labels must follow current revenue and contractual stage. HIVE's
  Q1 FY2027 remains predominantly digital-currency revenue; CLSK has a signed
  Sandersville lease with delivery expected from Q4 2027. The latter is more
  than an AI narrative, but not operating rent today. Update the role when
  revenue mix, funded construction or definitive contract evidence changes.
- Factory, GPU delivery, leased infrastructure and an expiring option have
  different clocks. Record the milestone, expected date, funding and a reason
  to reconsider for each position. Thesis failure or material timing changes
  falsify the original holding plan; stock price alone does not settle it.
- A fresh fetch can return old observations: 17/17 histories ended September 9
  on September 10. The report must expose that lag and cannot label these
  levels current entry quotes. Resume timing work when new observations arrive.
- Multiple tickers do not establish diversified risk. Use matched-session
  correlation as descriptive evidence and shared stress loss for construction.
  Neither establishes a stable future covariance matrix or optimal weights.
- Standard vertical legs must be matched before economic sizing. Preserve
  original per-leg warnings and distinguish complete spread payoff from the
  possibility of stock exposure through exercise/assignment.

## Accepted progress and limits

Accepted: dated source-backed milestone cards, reproducible position/price
calculations, explicit account-to-position loss scenarios, and a staged funding
decision process. No new empirical model edge, source-qualified paper outcome,
live realized profit, allocation authorization or execution authority was earned.
Do not repeat this unchanged snapshot as new progress.

Next useful evidence: post-event price/quote observations; confirmed funds and
closed-position outcomes; current valuation and diluted-share financing cases
for a supplier/infrastructure candidate versus an existing development holding.
