# Refresh completion before briefing

Belief: fixed start times alone do not establish a dependency. The old 07:30
Mountain brief preceded the 07:35 options refresh; three deployed service
copies lagged reviewed code. A zero exit code also cannot establish input
freshness.

Rule: complete the bounded research chain, validate source generation times
and references, and save hashes in a completion receipt. The guarded morning
brief checks the same-session receipt before work and before delivery. Missing,
failed, old, future or changed evidence blocks it. Never recover by removing
the guard or treating a historical email record as this run's success.

Measurement: deployed checksum drift count, adverse guard tests, and the next
session's completion/brief receipts. Duration is not monetary cost. A successful
refresh earns no accepted paper evidence or promotion credit.

Falsifier: a guarded delivery proceeds with invalid inputs at either check,
or schedule status reports a service hash different from reviewed code.

Limits: existing full-day calendar, app/model availability, quote-quality and
tracker limitations, and concurrent refreshes remain visible. This is a
research dependency repair; operator decisions and risk constants are intact.
