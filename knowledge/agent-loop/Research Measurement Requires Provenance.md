# Research Measurement Requires Provenance

Date: 2026-09-28
Owner: codex
Related: [[Cloud and Local Paper Ledger Ownership]]

Belief: diagnostic volume and report freshness are not validated investment
progress. Source qualification, costs, independent events and forward timing
must remain separate measurements.

Evidence: the frozen desk audit found 1 reconciled paper event and 0 with
reported fees, versus 1,025 closed shadow rows. Calibration reports 713 premature
settlements and 870 repeated exposures. Walk-forward/factor loaders saw zero
because they did not support the producer's `items` schema; repair exposes
1,021 scorable rows but does not cure their provenance.

Durable rule: test adapters against real producer shapes. Unknown costs keep net
return unknown. Separate reconciled fills, unverified paper estimates and shadow
proxies. Cluster descriptive intervals by event and withhold a single-event CI.
Historic cohorts registered after the fact are replay, not forward evidence.

Measurement: `./inferno research-audit` records source hashes, evidence gaps and
deltas in qualified events, known-cost fills, forward short-premium events and
issuer review debt. A completed audit is not accepted promotion progress. Keep
no-progress backoff active; adding reports does not justify more frequent loops.

Falsifier: source-backed cost-complete fills, prospective fixed-rule observations
and later outperformance of predeclared baselines would overturn the current
insufficiency conclusion. Neither synthetic labels nor backfilled features do.

Authority: no ticket decisions, risk inputs, evaluator thresholds or capital
permissions change. Flow-adjusted drawdown as a new gate input requires separate
operator acknowledgement after validation.
