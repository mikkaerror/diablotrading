## Beliefs, evidence and falsifiers

- **Builder provenance is too coarse for confidence.** All 183 market contexts
  are `fallback`, while 179 attached pulses identify Schwab daily history.
  The builder sets the label for ordinary row reconstruction. Falsifier: verify
  the origin, observation time and session for every *consumed* field; attached
  pulse coverage alone cannot justify upgrading confidence. Do not flip labels
  or remove penalties before the field-level comparison.
- **Coverage penalties are not company-risk evidence.** Only 40/183 rows link
  edge research; 124 get a missing-edge penalty after explicit exemptions.
  Falsifier: later same-universe outcomes show that this availability indicator
  adds independent predictive value after source/sector controls.
- **Selectivity cannot choose a threshold.** Readiness >=72 retains 86/183;
  adjusted conviction >=72 retains zero. Different definitions explain why the
  same number does not mean the same thing. Falsifier: a predeclared, later,
  after-cost comparison shows a proposed alternative improves outcomes and
  downside, including unselected names.
- **Read risk policy before alleging a bypass.** The actual risk function
  already handles thin liquidity and explicit liquidity-gate failure. Absence
  of a wide-spread flag proves neither valid quotes nor full admission.
